Two things worth knowing

The beach is cheaper than we had budgeted. The engine prints "Taxes included" and shows the VAT inside the total. Earlier planning grossed these quotes up by 21% on top, overstating the beach lodging by about $250 each. Do not gross up engine quotes.

The Airbnb cancellation terms are tighter than the badge suggests. Free for 24 hours after booking, then partial refund only until 22 December 2026. The badge is misleading. After 22 December there is no refund. Treat the deposit as committed once placed. Collect deposits first.

The stated preference cannot be met on the outbound. Mexico City is 4h50 nonstop and 7 to 8 hours connecting, so every afternoon departure from Detroit lands between 21:50 and 01:15. Checked flying out 28 December instead: same result, plus an extra Airbnb night. Decision is to keep the 08:40 nonstop, because 29 December is one of only two free evenings in Mexico City and trading it for $728 per person is a poor deal.

The one real risk on the outbound

Exactly one nonstop per day on roughly 130 seats, in holiday week, and we need 8 of them. Seat availability is the real risk, not fare. Book before watching the price.
