Two things worth knowing

The fourth room is the expensive decision, not the view. Six adults as two couples and two singles can be housed in three rooms or in four. Three rooms puts the two singles together in a Junior Suite Two Double, which is two separate beds and breaks no rule, at $966 each. Four rooms gives each single their own Junior Suite at $1,500 each. The fourth room is what costs money here. It is $534 more for each single, $1,067 to the group, which is more than four times what upgrading a couple from a standard suite to Ocean Front costs. The ocean view is the cheap part of this decision and the fourth room is the expensive part, which is the opposite of how it looks.

Mexico City is committed. Free for 24 hours after booking, then partial refund only until 16:00 on 22 December 2026, listing local time. The free window has already closed. Until 22 December only a partial refund is available and after that nothing, so the money is committed. The second payment of $2,251.42 lands on 14 December, eight days ahead of that cliff.

Be on the same Mexico City to Cancun flight

The one worth coordinating is Mexico City to Cancun on 3 January. Everybody continuing to the beach is on that leg, and being on the same aircraft means one arrival, one transfer and one van from the airport instead of six people trickling in. Aeromexico runs it hourly at a flat fare, so the time is a group decision rather than a price decision. If you are coming to the beach, say so before anybody ticketing starts.
