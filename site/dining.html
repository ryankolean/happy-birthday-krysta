The birthday, Saturday 2 January

Krysta turns 35. The one thing on the calendar is lunch, and it is booked. Everything after it is open on purpose.

Booked: Pujol, 14:30, table for 7

Confirmed 23 October 2026. Main dining room, Tennyson 133 in Polanco, about 4.7 km from Casa KM. The confirmation number and the terms are in the gitignored private/ folder.

Ryan's card is on the booking and takes the whole bill on the day. Everybody settles with him afterwards. Budget $200 to $250 for the food and expect to add drinks and tip on top of it.

Free to change until 14:30 on 1 January

Up to exactly 24 hours before, any change is free online through OpenTable. After that, a cancellation, a no-show, a date change or a cut to the diner count each cost MX$2,000 per person, about $824 across seven.

Two different penalties are published. OpenTable's confirmation says MX$2,000 per person. Pujol's own FAQ says a no-show is charged 100 US dollars per person. The booking was made through OpenTable, so the MX$2,000 figure is the one that governs it, but do not be surprised if the restaurant quotes the other.

Pujol also says their reservations team calls the day before to confirm attendance. Somebody needs to answer that call.

The headcount still has room

Pujol returns the same seatings at 6, 7 and 8 on this date, so the party can move freely inside that range right up to the deadline. At 9 or more there is no online availability at all, and a ninth guest would mean emailing the restaurant directly.

The menu is changing, and the price with it

Checked on pujol.com.mx on 27 October. The menus page still prints the Tasting Menu at MX$3,795, VAT included, which is the figure this trip is budgeted at. Their own FAQ, on the same site, describes something different:

  • The format stops being a tasting menu and becomes a five course restaurant menu with choices: snacks, cold starter, hot starter, main course, mole, dessert. Starter, main and dessert each pick from three options.
  • The price for it is MX$3,595, about $211.
  • The kitchen is working the Central Pacific region, which means Colima, Nayarit, Jalisco, Guerrero and Sinaloa, and the menu is mainly fish and shellfish.

No date is published for the switch. If it lands before 2 January the bill goes down by about $12 each, which is why the higher figure is the one budgeted. Nothing here needs acting on, but two things are worth knowing: choosing from three options per course is easier for a party of seven than a fixed tasting menu, and a seafood-led menu is not. Dietary restrictions have to be given in advance, so anybody who does not eat fish or shellfish should say so now rather than on the day.

Before going

  • No shorts, no sportswear. The dress code is casual formal.
  • 15 minute grace period.
  • No valet parking. Take a car and get dropped.
  • Minimum age 12, at full menu price.
  • Dietary restrictions in advance: reservaciones@pujol.com.mx, +52 55-5545-4111.

After lunch

Nothing is booked and nothing needs to be. Lunch ends around 17:00 in Polanco, which is the opposite end of the city from the house, so the real choice is whether to stay in Polanco for a while or head back toward Roma and Condesa.

Everything below is a suggestion, not a plan. It is a holiday Saturday, so check hours the week before rather than turning up.

Stay in Polanco for an hour or two

  • Avenida Presidente Masaryk starts a few minutes from the door. It is the expensive shopping street, and it is pleasant to walk whether or not anybody buys anything.
  • Parque Lincoln, a couple of blocks south, is the neighbourhood's own park and an easy place to sit while the lunch wears off.
  • Museo Soumaya and Museo Jumex share Plaza Carso, a short ride north. Soumaya is free and unmistakable from the outside. Jumex is contemporary art and reportedly stays open later on Saturdays than the rest of the week. Sources disagree on Jumex's admission, so check fundacionjumex.org and soumaya.com.mx nearer the time. Both are a plausible hour if the group wants to be somewhere other than a table.
  • Campomar Masaryk is on the map already if anybody wants small bites later rather than a drink.

Dinner

There is unlikely to be one. A two and a half hour lunch finishing at 17:00 is the big meal of the day. If anyone is hungry later, eat near the house rather than booking anything.

Why Pujol and not something else

Pujol holds two Michelin stars in the 2026 guide. Only one other restaurant in the country does, and Quintonil caps at six in its main dining room, so it could not take the group at all.

Of the eleven Michelin starred restaurants in Mexico City, Pujol was the only one confirmed able to seat seven on 2 January when the field was checked on 23 October. That is the whole reason. The rest of the field has been taken off this page now that the table is held.

One correction worth keeping, because it cost weeks: the eight tasting menu minimum that was treated here as the blocker applies to Pujol's private dining room, not to a regular table. A normal table for seven has no minimum, which is what got booked, so there is no wasted eighth cover and never had to be.
