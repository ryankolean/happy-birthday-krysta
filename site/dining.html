Birthday dinner, Saturday 2 January

Party of 7, down from 8 on 12 October. Hardest night of the week to seat, because it is a Saturday on a holiday weekend. The drop to 7 broke Pujol's minimum, which is covered below.

The constraint nobody plans around

The balloon still has a 4:30 AM pickup, but it no longer eats the whole day. Repriced for two on 23 October: three of the four operators land back between 09:45 and 11:30, not the 2 PM this page was written against. See the balloon options. A 7:30 PM dinner is now about fifteen hours after the alarm with a usable afternoon in the middle, rather than fifteen hours with nothing but a compulsory nap.

That softens the constraint without removing it. There are still two tasting menus in the itinerary counting whatever New Year's Eve becomes, and only two of the seven are getting up at 4:30.

So the real question is not which restaurant is best. It is whether this night wants a tasting menu at all.

Ruled out

Quintonil caps at 6. Main dining room takes 2 to 6, the kitchen counter 1 to 3, and they explicitly ask guests not to book multiple reservations intending to be seated together. For reference their window is two months ahead on the 1st at midnight, so January 2027 would open 1 November. Contact: +52 55 5280 2680, reservaciones@quintonil.com.

Pujol, the incumbent

Private dining room, capacity 8 to 14, minimum 8 tasting menus, and no room rental fee.

The group no longer meets the minimum. At 7 we either pay for an eighth cover nobody eats, roughly $223 wasted, or give up the private room and take a regular table if one exists. This is the single biggest consequence of dropping to 7, and it flips the recommendation toward Em, which takes parties of 3 to 8 and fits 7 with nothing to negotiate. MX$3,795 each, about $223 per person before drinks and tip. Seatings are 2:00 PM or 7:30 PM. The 2:00 PM is usable again now the balloon is two people back by about 11:15 rather than the whole group back at 2 PM, which was the stated reason for writing it off. The eight menu minimum is still broken at seven, so this reopens a seating, not the booking.

Cancellation is MX$2,000 each (about $118) inside 48 hours. Pujol does not book through OpenTable and its booking window is unknown, which makes it the biggest blind spot on the whole trip.

Em, the real peer

One Michelin star 2024 through 2026, number 71 in Latin America's 50 Best. Main dining room takes parties of 3 to 8, so 7 fits with nothing to negotiate and no minimum to pad. MX$3,900 each, about $229 per person, with a MX$2,000 deposit per person (about $118 each) due at booking.

Two things no write-up mentions:

Em has moved. The old Tonala 133 address in Roma is dead. It reopens October 2026 at Av. Amsterdam 76, Condesa, which is a walk from the Oasis rather than a ride to Polanco. Phone +52 55 6450 1302.

2 January is not bookable yet, and not sold out either. Tock returns "Em has not opened reservations for 2 ene." Probing the horizon: 12 and 19 December are open, 31 December and 1 January are not. They are holding the holiday week back. Same situation as Pujol, but with a published phone number and a confirmed 8 top.

The lower effort picks

The honest pick is Maximo Bistrot or Rosetta. One Michelin star between them, both a la carte so people order to their appetite, both walkable from the house so there is no car at either end, and both roughly a quarter of Pujol's price per head.

  • Maximo Bistrot, Avenida Alvaro Obregon 65 Bis, Roma. Private dining with notice. One Michelin star 2025. Window is one month out, not 90 days, so 2 January opens around 2 December. rsvp@maximobistrot.com.mx, +52 55 5525 2661.
  • Rosetta, in a Roma mansion. A la carte, dishes MX$240 to MX$610, about $50 to $88 each. One reviewer booked four months out for a party of 10 plus.

Worth a look either way

Balcon del Zocalo, 6th floor of the Zocalo Central Hotel, 5 de Mayo 61, facing the Cathedral. Tasting menu MX$1,850, about $109 each, less than half Pujol, with the cathedral view that was on Krysta's own list. Seasonal pairing MX$1,650. +52 55 5130 5134.

Sud 777 is the same MX$1,850 each for twelve courses but sits in Jardines del Pedregal, 35 to 45 minutes south. That kills it for this night.

Other nights

Every venue below is plotted on the map with its walk from the house.

  • 29 December: nothing to book. The lucha libre evening runs 17:45 to 20:45 and includes a full meal, so it is the dinner. If anyone is still hungry afterwards they eat near wherever the show lets out and then go home. No reservation, no bar.
  • 30 December: taco day. Casual only. Cuina on Tabasco 46 if anyone wants a table, CoverManager slug cuina-cdmx, max 10 online.
  • La Table Krug: Tuesday to Saturday, one seating at 8:00 PM. 29 December is now taken by lucha libre, so 30 December is the only night it fits, and that is taco day. Effectively out unless taco day moves. It is at the St. Regis either way, so do not book it if the group takes a St. Regis New Year's Eve.

Ling Ling is no longer spoken for on 29 December. It held that slot as the only open upscale seating of the week, but the booking was never actually made and the evening went to lucha libre on 23 October. It is now a 2 January candidate, where it matters more: Pujol's private room needs eight tasting menus and the group is seven. OpenTable, up to 20, released 30 September. Availability was last checked at a party of 8, so re-check at 7 before booking.
