Why a resort and not a residence

A single large residence split between six people only works by arguing over who takes the sofa bed. Dreams Jade is a conventional resort: every pair gets a room with a door, the board is all-inclusive so there is no food estimate to carry, and the two singles can have their own rooms without anybody negotiating over a living room.

Four rooms, four Kings, one category

DECIDED 2 November 2026. Four rooms, every one of them a Junior Suite with Ocean View, every one with a King bed. Two rooms for the two couples and one each for the two single travellers. All four booked in the same category and requested adjacent. No mixed categories, and that is now the resort's rule rather than a preference: they confirmed on 2 November that all four rooms must be in one category for the front desk to even attempt to place the group together. Any cheaper build buys a group scattered across the property. Quoted direct. Nothing is booked yet, and what the four rooms cost is on the costs page rather than here.

Why 3 January and not earlier

Unchanged from the previous property. The beach leg starts on 3 January because the Mexico City leg runs to that morning, and because holiday rate bands across the Riviera Maya end on 2 January.
