03Dreams Jade Resort & Spanot booked

Puerto Morelos, between Cancun and Playa del Carmen. 6 of us, as 2 couples and 2 singles. All-inclusive, scored 8 on 153 reviews. Repriced live on 2026-10-28.

Open the listing

Why a resort and not a residence

A single large residence split between six people only works by arguing over who takes the sofa bed. Dreams Jade is a conventional resort: every pair gets a room with a door, the board is all-inclusive so there is no food estimate to carry, and the two singles can have their own rooms without anybody negotiating over a living room.

The fourth room costs more than the ocean view

Six adults as two couples and two singles can be housed in three rooms or in four. Three rooms puts the two singles together in a Junior Suite Two Double, which is two separate beds and breaks no rule, at $966 each. Four rooms gives each single their own Junior Suite at $1,500 each. The fourth room is what costs money here. It is $534 more for each single, $1,067 to the group, which is more than four times what upgrading a couple from a standard suite to Ocean Front costs. The ocean view is the cheap part of this decision and the fourth room is the expensive part, which is the opposite of how it looks.

Four rooms, every one of them a Junior Suite with Ocean View. Both couples take an Ocean View King, each single takes an Ocean View King on the single-occupancy rate. Four separate reservations are four independent prices, so one couple buying up does not raise anybody else's rate by a cent. What mixing categories does cost is adjacency: Ocean Front rooms sit in the buildings nearest the water and standard Junior Suites are set back, so a group spread across categories is not a group in the same corridor. Putting everyone in the same category fixes that, and it costs $89 each more than buying one couple an Ocean Front and leaving everyone else in a standard room. It is also a far easier thing to put in a group chat.

Never put a single in an ocean front room

A solo traveller pays 80 percent of the two-person rate on the four cheapest room types and 100 percent of it on everything above them. From Preferred Club Tropical View upward, including every Ocean Front and every Preferred Club room, one person pays the full two-person price for a phantom second guest. So the two singles go in an Ocean View room or below. Never put a single in an Ocean Front or a Preferred Club room. Verified at one adult and at six adults across four rooms on 28 October.

Booking quotes this property before tax

Booking.com prints "Included: City tax" and "Excluded: 16% VAT" on every rate at this property. Every figure in room_catalogue and combinations therefore carries both numbers: quoted_usd is what Booking displays, all_in_usd is that times 1.16. Combinations and the budget use all_in only. An earlier beach quote on this trip ran the other way, already tax-inclusive, and was grossed up anyway, overstating the lodging by about $250 each. Here the tax is genuinely excluded and must be added. Check which way round a quote is before doing arithmetic.

Food is in the rate now

The rate is all-inclusive, so meals, drinks and gratuities are in it. That is why the beach leg carries no food estimate. The earlier room-only plan for this leg added $324 each on top of the lodging line for eating in Puerto Morelos village. Judge this on total outlay rather than on the lodging line alone. The recommended build is $1,534 each all in on the flexible rate and $1,309 on the non-refundable one, against $1,435 for the room-only plan it replaced. So flexible is $99 each more and non-refundable is $126 each less, and either buys a private room and an ocean view for every person plus no decisions about dinner.

Four rooms is not a group, and there is a better lever

Four rooms is not a group. Hyatt Inclusive Collection group desks start at ten rooms, so there is no group rate to ask for and no group discount being withheld. Anyone who offers one is selling something else.

Hyatt's advisor-only booking program. Dreams is in it and the five nights clear its four-night minimum. Reported benefits are a property credit of roughly $100 per stay, a room upgrade confirmed at booking, a welcome amenity, and early check-in and late checkout on availability. Four reservations may mean four credits. Every source for this is a travel agency or affiliate blog rather than Hyatt itself. Get the credit and the upgrade in writing before paying. The Prive rate tracks the flexible rate, so it is not comparable to the non-refundable figures on this page.

Why 3 January and not earlier

Unchanged from the previous property. The beach leg starts on 3 January because the Mexico City leg runs to that morning, and because holiday rate bands across the Riviera Maya end on 2 January.
