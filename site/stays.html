Recommended: flexible room-only. Free cancellation, refundable until 13 December 2026. A further 5% ($226) comes off through their loyalty programme, which offered at the room selection step for a prior Fives booking, or on signup

Room only or all inclusive

All-inclusive works out to $84.30 per adult per night at the current discount, so full board costs only $98 more each than room-only plus eating in the village. That gap used to be $239 each. Break-even is about two resort cocktails a day. Room-only is still the pick given the 16 minute village walk, but it is now close enough to put to the group.

Why 3 January and not earlier

  • The Fives runs a holiday rate band 24 Dec 2026 to 2 Jan 2027, with the next band starting exactly 3 January
  • The identical unit is $7,895 for 29 Dec to 3 Jan versus $4,990 for 3 to 8 Jan
  • A stay including 2 January has no all-inclusive board available at all

Confirmed, so nobody has to ask again

  • No resort fee
  • No reservation fee
  • No New Year's Eve gala charge
  • Only holiday charge is a 25 December gala at $100 per adult, which 3 January clears by nine days
Still to confirm before booking
  • Environmental tax at check-in, sources conflict between under $25 for the stay and up to $270
  • Whether the 32% rate survives to booking and stacks with the 5% loyalty discount
  • Kitchen items surcharge if anyone plans to cook
