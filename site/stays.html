03Why we are leaving Oasis in Roma Norte

It held the Mexico City slot until 2026-10-12. Its own reviews are what moved us off it.

Open the old listing

  • Guests report no windows in most bedrooms. One: "no windows in most of the rooms, which means very little airflow unless you leave your room door open." Another: "no A/C so the upstairs rooms get hot. no windows so there is no airflow but there were tower fans in all rooms."
  • Two separate reviews raise the smell. "The house smells very musty, and so do the towels and bedding. You can't rest because of the smell." A second lists "visible mold on ceiling of some bedrooms" and "occasional sewage smell by lower common area."
  • A guest notes "there is no real kitchen appliances to use", which the New Year's Eve plan depends on.
  • The street-facing first bedroom has road noise and a street lamp with no curtains. One guest bought and installed curtains during their stay.
  • At 7 guests it reprices to $3,996, or $571 each, the most expensive option on the board.
What it did have

Six bedrooms and 6.5 bathrooms, a private rooftop, no quiet hours, and 4.91 from 145 reviews. The bathroom count and the rooftop are the two things Casa KM had to match, and it does.

Recommended: flexible room-only. Free cancellation, refundable until 13 December 2026. A further 5% ($226) comes off through their loyalty programme, which offered at the room selection step for a prior Fives booking, or on signup

Room only or all inclusive

All-inclusive works out to $84.30 per adult per night at the current discount, so full board costs only $98 more each than room-only plus eating in the village. That gap used to be $239 each. Break-even is about two resort cocktails a day. Room-only is still the pick given the 16 minute village walk, but it is now close enough to put to the group.

Why 3 January and not earlier

  • The Fives runs a holiday rate band 24 Dec 2026 to 2 Jan 2027, with the next band starting exactly 3 January
  • The identical unit is $7,895 for 29 Dec to 3 Jan versus $4,990 for 3 to 8 Jan
  • A stay including 2 January has no all-inclusive board available at all

Confirmed, so nobody has to ask again

  • No resort fee
  • No reservation fee
  • No New Year's Eve gala charge
  • Only holiday charge is a 25 December gala at $100 per adult, which 3 January clears by nine days
Still to confirm before booking
  • Environmental tax at check-in, sources conflict between under $25 for the stay and up to $270
  • Whether the 32% rate survives to booking and stacks with the 5% loyalty discount
  • Kitchen items surcharge if anyone plans to cook
Deposits

Both legs want money before they are held, and Casa KM stops refunding entirely after 22 December. Collect from everyone first. The full cancellation position is on the costs page, and the booking deadlines are on the to do list.
