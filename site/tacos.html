    The route

      Suggestions

      Every taco place on the map that is not on your route, cheapest first: the extra walking it costs to slot it in at its best position. Hours are checked at the time you would arrive on Wednesday Wed 30 Dec, and for Thursday Thu 31 Dec in case the day slips.

        Before anyone goes
        • Cash in small bills, about 300 to 400 MXN a person for the day. Stands and canasta are cash only.
        • Los Parados is standing only. Split into two groups of four and order in rounds.
        • Avoid 31 December, when many places close in the afternoon, and 1 January.
        • Re-check every stop on Instagram or Maps on 28 December. No stop publishes holiday hours.
