Hot air balloon, 2 January

Researched 23 October 2026. Nothing is booked. Every figure below was taken by selecting the date and party size on the operator's own listing, not from a teaser price.

Two things changed

It is two passengers, not seven. The balloon is now Krysta and Ryan only. The rest of the group has that Saturday morning free, which the itinerary does not yet reflect. The budget still carries this line at seven.

Weight is the deciding constraint, not price. It outranks rating, duration and cost, and it is the reason nothing here is bookable from the picker.

The weight problem

Three of the four listings publish a policy. These come off the listings themselves rather than a reseller:

Listing What it says
Top-Rated balloon + breakfast at La Gruta "Weights over 100kg pays 2 usd per kilo."
Balloon ride and breakfast at La Gruta "Weights over 100kg pays 2 USD per kilo (General Policy of Balloonport)"
Sunrise Balloon & Private Transport "Standard weight limit per passenger: 100 kg (220 lbs)." Phrased as a limit, not a surcharge.
Unforgettable Hot Air Balloon Ride Says nothing at all, which is worse than saying something.

Both of our passengers are over the 100 kg threshold and one is materially over it. Exact declared figures are in private/, because this page is group facing. Under the USD 2 per kilo rule the surcharge for the pair is roughly $117, which is an irritation rather than a problem.

The problem is the ceiling, not the surcharge. Operator sites publish hard maximums well below where we sit:

  • Mexitours: 125 kg maximum per person, a per-kilo supplement from 101 to 110 kg, and 50 percent of the fare above 110 kg
  • Aerodiverti: 50 percent of the fare from 100 kg up
  • Power Traveller: 90 kg per person
  • Guides and resellers variously quote 99, 100, 120 and 125 kg

The surcharge rate is quoted as MXN 30, MXN 35, MXN 40, USD 3 and USD 25 per kilo across these pages. Those cannot all be true, and at USD 25 per kilo the pair would owe roughly $1,465 rather than $117. Treat every third-party figure as unverified.

Teotihuacan sits at roughly 2,300 m. Thin air produces less lift, which is why payload limits there are tighter than sea-level ballooning would suggest.

Operators also require real declared weights at booking for flight planning. Several say the excess is collected at reception, and that it can delay boarding.

This settles whether the balloon operates that day. It does, on four separate operators, which was listed as an open question and as the single largest piece of provisional spending on the birthday.

Why a private flight, not a shared basket

On a shared flight we are one payload constraint among as many as sixteen strangers, and we are the ones who get bumped. On a private flight the operator plans the envelope around exactly two passengers, so it is both the likeliest yes and the better birthday.

Expect private to cost well above the shared figures. One operator quotes MX$1,990 per person shared against MX$3,900 private, so budget for roughly double until a real quote comes back.

The Top-Rated listing is the one to ask. It advertises private pricing on the 04:30 slot on 2 January and carries by far the strongest record at 4.97 from 8,319 ratings, against 4,338, 3,280 and 307 for the others.
