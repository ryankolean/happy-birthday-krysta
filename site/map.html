What basing in Del Valle Norte costs in walking

30 of 117 places are within a 2.5 km walk and 82 sit inside the opening view. From the Oasis it was 48 of 67, because that house stood inside the Roma and Condesa restaurant cluster instead of 2.7 km south of it.

Concretely: Panaderia Rosetta goes from a 4 minute walk at the Oasis to a ride, and Bar Latosa, Margot, Ruido Bar, Pandora Rooftop all stop being walks. Budget for rides in the evenings: an Uber across that gap runs roughly MX$60 to MX$90.

These walks are a worst case, not the real ones. The pin is the middle of Del Valle Norte rather than the house, on purpose, and it sits about half a kilometre further from Roma than the flat actually does. Measured from the listing's own circle, 27 of 117 are inside a 2.5 km walk rather than 30. Treat the figures below as the pessimistic end until the booking is confirmed.

Append to data/places.yml with a name, category and address, then run npm run geocode and npm run build. Coordinates are cached in data/geocache.json, so nothing already resolved gets looked up twice.
