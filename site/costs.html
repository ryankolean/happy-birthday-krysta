Riviera Maya

The Fives Oceanfront Puerto Morelos, 6 people as 2 couples and 2 singles. Quoted live on the resort's own engine on 2026-10-27, taxes included, nothing booked.

One residence does not sleep this group

The Two Bedroom Oceanview Residence has four sleeping surfaces, not three. Bedroom one is a king. Bedroom two holds two queen beds and has its own bathroom and its own balcony. The living room then adds a double sofa bed, because the unit is described as a One Bedroom Partial Oceanview Residence with a second bedroom bolted on, and that one bedroom layout carries a sofa bed. So one unit sleeps six adults, which is its published maximum, but only by putting two of them in the living room. Two couples and two singles means a couple on the sofa bed, or a single on it, whichever way it is cut. That is the problem, and it is one bed short rather than three.

Give the second bedroom to the two singles. It has two queen beds, a private bathroom and its own balcony, so each of them gets a real bed and they share a room that is effectively a self contained wing. Put one couple in the king, and buy one small unit for the other couple. A Junior Suite is the cheapest way to do that and nobody touches the sofa bed. This is the correction that matters: the earlier version of this plan put the singles in the Junior Suite, where one of them would have taken its sofa bed. Same two units, same money, materially worse. The second bedroom of the residence is the best room on the property for two people travelling alone, and it was being given to a couple who did not need it.
