Splitting it

  • Agree a per-person target before booking
  • Lodging is the only line billed as a whole. Both are split evenly by head.
  • Keep the two legs on separate ledgers. Guests who leave after Mexico City should not carry beach costs.
  • Anyone joining only for the beach should not carry New Year's Eve.
  • Under room-only at the beach, food is individual rather than prepaid. Agree up front whether meals go on one card and get split, or people pay their own.
  • Note who fronts which booking and how they are repaid
  • Splitwise or similar, agreed up front
