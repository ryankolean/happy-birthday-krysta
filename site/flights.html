Detroit and Cancun share a clock, Mexico City does not

Across these dates Detroit is on EST and Cancun never leaves UTC-5, so those two read the same. Mexico City sits an hour behind both. That is why the outbound looks like a 3 hour 50 minute hop on the clock when it is really 4 hours 50 in the air, and why the flight home from Cancun reads honestly.

05The Cancun leg, and the bag trap

Be on the same aircraft

Six people are on this leg and it is the one that pays to share. One arrival at CUN means one transfer and one van to Puerto Morelos instead of six people trickling in across the afternoon. Aeromexico runs it roughly hourly at a flat fare, so the departure time is a group decision and not a price decision. The 13:55 is the current pick because it leaves after the 11:00 Casa KM checkout.

Read this before you book the Cancun hop

Tell everybody the same two things before they book, because the default choice is the wrong one. Buy the Basic fare, not the cheapest one shown. The cheapest fare is Light, and it carries no checked bag and no carry-on at all, so it strands you with a personal item on the way to a five night beach stay. Then buy the checked bag online before check-in, at 500 MXN, roughly 27 USD. It costs more at the airport. Basic plus a bag is about 222 per person. The first fare that bundles a bag is Flex at 262, so buying the bag separately saves about 40 each and 242 across the six. Nobody should rely on Ryan's Delta status here: Aeromexico excludes Basic purchases on domestic routes from SkyTeam and Delta member baggage benefits. Full working in the flights table under the to_beach leg.

Do not book the outbound as one group

Do not try to put the group on one booking. The Delta nonstop will not sell to a party of five or more in a single transaction, and every passenger added prices the whole booking at the highest bucket reached. Book in pairs against the same flight number. See party_size_ceiling in the flights table.

06What to send Ryan

For each leg you have booked
  • Airline and flight number for each leg
  • Date of each leg
  • Airport codes, origin and destination, because MEX and NLU are both Mexico City
  • Scheduled departure time, local to the departure airport
  • Scheduled arrival time, local to the arrival airport
  • Whether the booking is ticketed or still a hold
  • Do not ask for confirmation numbers in a group thread
Not confirmation numbers

Flight numbers and times are fine to share and fine to keep here. Record locators are not: this page is generated from a file in a git repository, and anybody holding a locator and a surname can change or cancel a booking. Keep yours to yourself.
