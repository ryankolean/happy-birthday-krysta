05The Cancun leg, and the bag trap

Be on the same aircraft

Six people are on this leg and it is the one that pays to share. One arrival at CUN means one transfer and one van to Puerto Morelos instead of six people trickling in across the afternoon. Settled on 1 November: the 14:55, landing 18:36. Aeromexico runs the route roughly hourly at a flat fare, so the time was never a price decision, but it turned out to be a baggage one. AM Plus is the only fare family here that includes a checked bag and it is not sold on the 13:55, which was the earlier pick.

Read this before you book the Cancun hop

Book the 14:55 and buy AM Plus Classic, 260.25. Not the cheapest fare shown, and not Main Cabin at all. The reason is baggage. No Main Cabin fare on this route includes a checked bag. Not Basic, not Classic, and not Flex, which is the expensive one. Every one of them charges for it separately, so the real Main Cabin price is 223.15 plus about 27 for a bag bought online, roughly 250. AM Plus Classic is 260.25 with the bag and an extra-legroom seat already in it. Ten dollars, and nothing left to buy at the airport. Do not be tempted by the cheapest fare on the page. That is Light, and it carries no checked bag and no carry-on at all, not even a bin item, which strands you with a personal item on the way to a five night beach stay. AM Plus is not sold on the 13:55, which is why the group moved an hour later. Nobody should rely on Ryan's Delta status here: Aeromexico excludes Basic purchases on domestic routes from SkyTeam and Delta member baggage benefits. Full working in the flights table under the to_beach leg.

No Main Cabin fare includes a checked bag

Main Cabin was the plan until the fares were read properly. Not one of its four fares includes a checked bag. Every row of the comparison reads "Checked baggage 55 lb, Additional charge", Flex included, which is what the earlier 262 claim got wrong. That makes the real Main Cabin price 223.15 plus a bag, and at that point AM Plus Classic is 10 more and strictly better.

Why the Cancun hop moved an hour later

The plan was the 13:55 until 1 November. AM Plus is not sold on that departure at any passenger count, nor on the 08:55: the column reads "Not available". The 14:55 is the first departure after the 11:00 checkout that sells it. The cost is one hour. The group lands 18:36 instead of 17:36 and reaches Puerto Morelos around 19:15, which is still a reasonable arrival for a leg with nothing scheduled on it.

Do not book the outbound as one group

Do not try to put the group on one booking. The Delta nonstop will not sell to a party of five or more in a single transaction, and every passenger added prices the whole booking at the highest bucket reached. Book in pairs against the same flight number. See party_size_ceiling in the flights table.

Not confirmation numbers

Flight numbers and times are fine to share and fine to keep here. Record locators are not: this page is generated from a file in a git repository, and anybody holding a locator and a surname can change or cancel a booking. Keep yours to yourself.
