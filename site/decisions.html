Decision log

What was decided, and what it beat. Newest first.

Riviera Maya over a second city

Decided. Mexico City for New Year's Eve and the birthday, then Puerto Morelos to decompress. Both anchors happen in the city, so the beach leg carries no scheduling pressure.

The Fives Oceanfront over The Fives Beach

Decided, against the price. The Fives Beach Hotel and Residences priced lower, at $6,434 for the whole unit all-inclusive for a true 3BR with 3 baths, and it had 3BR inventory for the exact dates while the Oceanfront's 3BR was sold out. Chose the Oceanfront anyway for the boutique character and the walkable village. Worth knowing the decision cost money rather than saved it.

Beach leg arrives 3 January, not 29 December

Decided, and the dates are load bearing. The Fives runs a holiday rate band from 24 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, with the next band starting exactly 3 January. The identical unit is $7,895 for 29 December to 3 January versus $4,990 for 3 to 8 January, both whole-unit prices. A stay that includes 2 January has no all-inclusive board available at all.

Oasis 6BR over five boutique hotel rooms

Decided. The bed configuration was the binding constraint: three couples plus two singles who need separate rooms. Every Roma Norte and Condesa boutique offers a single king, which forces five rooms and adds roughly $375 to $440 each.

The Oasis solves it with six bedrooms and one spare, adds a private rooftop for midnight, has no quiet hours, and costs $491 each, $3,927 for the whole house.

It also corrected a much larger error. An earlier pass had quoted $1,790 to $2,340 each for Mexico City lodging. Colima 71's own direct rate for an ordinary week in February 2027 back-solves to about the same per-room-night as its New Year quote, and Roma Norte hotels were running active discounts into the holiday window. Nobody discounts into a demand spike. The genuine holiday premium is 0 to 20 percent, not the 10x the quote implied. The rest was the decision to buy five boutique rooms at roughly $572 a night.

Keep the 08:40 nonstop out of Detroit

Decided, against a stated preference. The group wanted early-to-mid afternoon departures. On DTW to MEX that is impossible: the route is 4h50 nonstop and 7 to 8 hours connecting, so every afternoon departure lands between 21:50 and 01:15. Flying out 28 December instead does not fix it and adds a night.

29 December is one of only two free evenings in Mexico City. Trading it for $728 each is a poor deal on a trip already spending $223 a head on one dinner. One 6 AM alarm across ten days.

Fly home 8 January, not 7

Corrected. The original Google Flights search returned CUN to DTW on 7 January, which is a four night beach stay. 8 January costs the same $493 on two of the three flights, so fly the 8th and keep the fifth night.

Taco day moved to 30 December, Sabores food tour dropped

Decided. Krysta asked for a day of just eating at taco trucks. 1 January is the only otherwise-free day and it is the worst possible choice, because many places close after lunch on the 31st and do not reopen until the 2nd.

30 December works, and swapping the guided Sabores tour for a self-guided crawl saves about $115 each and is closer to the actual request. It also keeps 29 December free for Ling Ling, which matters because 29 December is the only open upscale slot in the week.

Balloon on 2 January, or not at all

Ryan's call. Sunrise balloon over Teotihuacan on the birthday itself, or skip it.

That decision makes the weather terms critical, because 2 January has no rebooking slot: on the 3rd the group flies at 13:55 and a balloon day does not return until about 14:00.

Volare is the operator, chosen specifically for one published sentence: weather cancellations may be rescheduled or cancelled with no extra fee. WE FLY publishes no refund terms at all and Sky Balloons publishes no cancellation policy and appears to require self-transport to their airport for a 6 AM appointment. Volare's family private basket is MX$3,600 per person, takes 2 to 16, includes hotel pickup, and cancels free to 48 hours.

Side benefit: the package includes Teotihuacan entry, which closes a gap the itinerary could not otherwise fit.

New Year's Eve stays at the house

Approach decided, orders not placed. A potluck pickup run, with a private chef as the zero-effort fallback. The gala is demoted.

Driven by the street closures rather than by cost: Reforma shuts in both directions, the free party draws about 250,000, and midnight is already on the Oasis rooftop. A restaurant forces a choice between leaving dinner early and missing midnight. See nye.md.

Quintonil is out

Settled 2 October 2026. It caps at 6 in the main dining room and 1 to 3 at the kitchen counter, and it explicitly asks guests not to book multiple tables intending to be seated together. Earlier notes had this as disputed because every scrape of its Tock page returned a Cloudflare interstitial. It is not disputed.

The Fives quotes include tax

Settled 2 October 2026. Two research passes had contradicted each other on this, and the gross-up won. It was wrong.

The rate-selection page prints the breakdown directly: subtotal $3,739, VAT 21% $785, total $4,525, taxes included. Every beach figure that had been grossed up by 21% was overstated by about 17%, roughly $250 each on the lodging line.

Worth remembering why the error survived: a Booking.com figure agreed with the grossed-up total to within 0.8 percent, which looked like independent corroboration. It was not. The arithmetic was fine and the interpretation was wrong, and matching numbers cannot tell those apart.

Room-only over all-inclusive at the beach, narrowly

Recommended, not locked. At the current 32 percent discount, all-inclusive is $84.30 per adult per night, so full board costs only $98 more each than room-only plus eating in the village. That gap used to be $239 each.

Break-even is about $19.57 per adult per day, roughly two resort cocktails. Room-only still wins on the merits, because the village is a 16 minute walk with 19 restaurants inside a kilometre and the resort has three on-site venues with documented closure problems. But it is close enough now that the group should be asked rather than told.
